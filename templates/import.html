Format 2 — Key/value blocks

Blocks separated by ---. Title: and SQL: are required.

Title: Get active users
Description: Fetches users active in the last 30 days
Tags: analytics, users
State: Maharashtra
SQL:
SELECT * FROM users WHERE last_active > NOW() - INTERVAL '30 days';
---
Title: Revenue by month
Tags: finance
SQL:
SELECT DATE_TRUNC('month', created_at), SUM(amount) FROM orders GROUP BY 1;

State names are fuzzy-matched automatically in both formats — misspellings like "Kerela" or "Maharastra" are handled.
